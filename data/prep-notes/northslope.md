## Critical metric check

Before the interview, make sure you can define both metrics.

In standard ML terminology:

* Precision = true positives divided by all predicted positives.
* “False-positive rate” may mean false positives divided by actual negatives, which uses a different denominator.

Therefore, 22% precision and 60% false-positive rate can coexist, but only if they are calculated differently. Do not say “60% of alerts were false positives” unless that is genuinely what the metric meant. Say:

> “Precision was 22%, while the measured false-positive rate was 60% under the bank’s existing evaluation definition.”

Then explain the denominators if asked.
