The OpenAI Deployment Company to acquire Northslope
Northslope will bring best-in-class Forward Deployed Engineers, or FDEs, to meet rising enterprise demand for AI systems built for critical work.
Today we’re announcing that The OpenAI Deployment Company has agreed to acquire Northslope, an applied AI company that helps enterprises realize the full value of AI across their most important operations. Northslope builds on The OpenAI Deployment Company’s recent acquisition of Tomoro, bringing together hundreds of FDEs to help the world’s most important companies turn AI into results faster.

The OpenAI Deployment Company was launched earlier this year to help customers put frontier AI to use inside their businesses every day. As a committed partnership between OpenAI and 19 leading global investment firms, consultancies, and system integrators, it brings the scale and hands-on focus enterprise deployment requires, from choosing the right processes to meeting real-world security and governance requirements. 

Northslope specializes in this kind of embedded deployment work, delivering AI solutions for how businesses actually operate. The team supports mission-critical use cases across sectors, including faster cancer detection in healthcare, fewer supply-chain bottlenecks in manufacturing, and more renewable megawatts on the grid for energy producers. 

Around the world, Northslope FDEs work side by side with frontline operators to build production AI systems that deliver measurable bottom-line impact. Signaling the strength of the Northslope deployment team, it was the first member of the Palantir Vanguard: Elite network. Northslope revenue grew 7x in 2025 and now supports customers across the US, Europe, and the Middle East. 

Demand for enterprise AI deployment is accelerating

More than 2 million businesses now use OpenAI, double from a year ago, and enterprise momentum continues to build. With over 5 million weekly active users, Codex is showing how valuable agentic AI can be for enterprises. As models like GPT‑5.6 become more capable and efficient, that value will continue to grow.

The OpenAI Deployment Company is already working with enterprises across financial services, energy, software, and industrials, on AI systems for critical operations. Building on the acquisition of Tomoro, Northslope will significantly increase our deployment capacity to hundreds of FDEs. Together, the expanded team will work with business leaders and frontline operators to identify the workflows that power their businesses, find efficiencies, and build AI systems for long-term growth.  

What’s next

The acquisition is subject to customary closing conditions, including receipt of required regulatory approvals. Until closing, The OpenAI Deployment Company and Northslope will remain separate and independent companies.

After closing, the Northslope team will join The OpenAI Deployment Company, expanding our ability to help customers deploy AI in core operations while meeting the security, governance, and reliability requirements of real-world use. Upon closing, Northslope as a part of The OpenAI Deployment Company may also support customers on select joint commercial engagements driving frontier AI deployments in partnership with Palantir.
