Interview Overview

The interview has four phases:

Phase 1: Discovery Conversation (10 minutes)
We'll dive into a business problem together. Your job is to ask clarifying questions to understand what the customer really needs—not just what they ask for. Think of this like the beginning of a consulting engagement where you probe for hidden requirements and constraints.

What we're looking for:
How you uncover the real problem vs. the surface request
Whether you think about business context and impact
How you identify success criteria and constraints
Your ability to ask insightful questions
Phase 2: Solution Design & Architecture (5–10 minutes)
Once you understand the problem, you'll sketch out your proposed solution. What would you build? How would you use AI to solve this? What's your high-level architecture?

What we're looking for:
How naturally you think about where AI agents and LLMs fit
Whether you design AI-native solutions or traditional + AI bolt-ons
How clearly you articulate the "why" behind your approach
Whether your thinking is grounded in the customer's actual needs
Phase 3: Live Prototyping (50 minutes)
You'll build a working proof-of-concept using any coding agent you're comfortable with — Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor, or whatever you prefer. We're not grading polish; we're watching how you:

Direct the AI tool to build what you envision
Review and iterate on what it generates
Make smart tradeoffs between speed and quality
Stay focused on proving the core concept
You can use any languages, frameworks, or libraries you're familiar with.

What we're looking for:
How fluently you use AI tools as a force multiplier
Whether you validate what the AI generates
How you iterate based on what you learn
Your ability to explain your thinking and decisions
Phase 4: Presentation & Discussion (15–20 minutes)
You'll walk us through your prototype and explain:

What the prototype proves
Why this approach solves the customer's problem
What's still needed for production
Any tradeoffs you made and why
What we're looking for:
How clearly you communicate technical concepts to any audience
Whether you understand the full scope of building for production
Your ability to articulate business value, not just technical merit
How you think about handing off to an engineering team
