## Role shape and likely questions

Senior PM at an AI-forward company probably means:

- Shipping under ambiguity with partial specs
- Translating between technical deployment and business outcomes
- Coaching or enabling others without formal reporting lines
- Managing cost, quality, and adoption as the success metrics

Expect questions about scope decisions, tradeoffs under pressure, and how you move work forward when the brief is incomplete.
