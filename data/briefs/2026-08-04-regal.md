# Now: this interview

## Goal for this call

Confirm the role is manager of deployment, not pure strategy, map the Kinage and FDE story onto their motion, learn the loop and timeline, and leave them wanting the next round.

## Talking points

1. This is your shape of job. Own kickoff through go-live across multiple launches; coach technical delivery people; turn field pain into playbooks and roadmap signal.
2. Speed to value. You have shipped under show-impact-fast pressure. Regal's two-week value bar is a feature, not a threat. Explain how you would define the first win metric with a customer.
3. Playbooks over heroics. Going from 0 to 12 institutions and a cost-to-serve drop is evidence you scale deployment, not just survive it.
4. Quality bar for agents in production. Eval, precision, and false-positive work translates cleanly to voice agents that must be monitored, improved, and trusted.
5. Commercial mind. Deployments unlock renewals and expansion. Treat go-live as the start of the revenue relationship, not a project closeout.

## Opening, about 20 seconds

"The way I read the role: each customer launch is a product launch. I've been closest to that as a forward-deployed PM, owning delivery quality, coaching the work, and feeding what we learn back into a repeatable motion. Curious how Regal splits strategy versus day-to-day deployment ownership on this team."

1. How big is the FDE team today, and what does good management look like here in the first 90 days?
2. What is the biggest failure mode on launches right now: integrations, agent quality, customer readiness, or internal coordination?
3. What does the rest of the interview loop look like, who would I meet next, and what are you still testing for after this call?
