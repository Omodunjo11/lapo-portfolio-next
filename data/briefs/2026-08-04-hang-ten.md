# Now: this interview

## Goal for this call

Learn the real role shape, show that you are someone who ships in messy enterprise environments, and leave with a clear next step plus a readable map of who else you would meet.

## Talking points

1. You have lived the services versus product tension. Kinage and bank work: making AI delivery repeatable so it does not become expensive custom labor at scale, directly relevant if Hang Ten is reinventing IT services with AI.
2. Forward-deployed instinct. Embedded with clients, owned outcomes, turned tribal knowledge into playbooks (0 to 12 institutions, cost to serve down 62 percent).
3. Trust and evaluation, not demo theater. Precision and false-positive work shows you care about production quality bars; enterprises will punish flashy agents that fail.
4. Ambiguity comfort. Founding-team stage means incomplete briefs; you move anyway. Pair this with your incomplete-info story.
5. Why this wave, why now. You are not chasing "AI" for its own sake. You are chasing the gap between companies that can use AI to change how software runs, and everyone else.

## Opening, about 20 seconds

"I'm drawn to Hang Ten because you're attacking the actual enterprise software lifecycle, build, change, run, with AI, not just bolting a chatbot on. I've spent my recent work making AI delivery survive real volume without turning into a services sink. Curious how you're drawing that line here."
