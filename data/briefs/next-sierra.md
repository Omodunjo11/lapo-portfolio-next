## Stories to have ready

### Kinage: 0 to 12 institutions, cost-to-serve down 62 percent

Situation: Deployed AI across multiple bank customers. Each had different infra, compliance gates, and cost tolerance. Scaling required playbooks, not heroics.

Decision: Built repeatable deployment templates, coached technical teams on-site, measured cost per deployment, and fed failure modes back to product. Owned the system, not every transaction.

Result: 12 institutions live. Cost-to-serve dropped 62 percent. Teams could ship without me in the room. That's leverage.

### Bank eval gates and production quality

Situation: Regulated customer demanded precision gates before production. Incomplete specs, tight timeline, high stakes.

Decision: Cut scope to the gates that mattered most. Shipped a smaller, measurable first version. Measured adoption and failure modes in the field.

Result: Precision gates held. Failures fed back to product. Customer trusted the next release. Incomplete info is not an excuse; it's the constraint you work within.

### TD pushback and calm alternatives

Situation: TD wanted faster delivery on a timeline I thought was risky. Pressure to commit without full clarity.

Decision: Didn't say no. Offered a phased approach: ship core, measure, iterate. Spoke the risk and the outcomes, not the emotion.

Result: Delivery protected. TD saw the trade-offs. We shipped on time and learned what mattered.
