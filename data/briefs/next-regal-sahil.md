## 5 lines to land (practice out loud)

1. **People leadership:** "I manage deployed engineers like a launch org: clear weekly outcomes, a shared quality bar, daily unblock, and I take customer thrash so they keep focus."
2. **Systems:** "If the same failure shows up twice, it becomes a playbook step or a Product ticket — not another ad hoc fire drill."
3. **Playbook of excellence:** "Excellence is a checklist the team will use under pressure: kickoff metric, quality gate, go-live criteria, weekly executive status. If they ignore it, it’s not a playbook yet."
4. **Executive structure:** "Executives get a short consistent cadence: status, risk, ask. No surprise red. No complaint theater — options and owners."
5. **Customer / revenue:** "I’ll run the team and still jump into a customer when the revenue or trust risk needs a senior owner. Deployment ownership includes the commercial relationship after go-live."

## Stories to have ready (pick on his prompt)

**A. People + leverage (4–6 style)**  
Kinage / multi-account: coach technical delivery; playbooks; 0→12; cost-to-serve −62%. Emphasize *team leverage*, not "I did the tickets."

**B. Systems + playbook of excellence**  
Recurring custom heroics → named pattern → minimal playbook → run twice → default. Same answer as "how I create process."

**C. Protect team + customer confidence**  
Pressure → one win metric + quality gate; you own the no; daily honest status. Shock absorber for thrash.

**D. Executive presence beat**  
TD pushback: calm alternative to bad architecture; protect delivery; speak in outcomes and risk, not emotion.

**E. Frontline → Product + revenue**  
Bank eval loop: field failure modes → gates Product must respect; trust in production unlocks continued / expanded use.
